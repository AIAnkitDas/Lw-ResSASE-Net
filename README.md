# Lw-ResSASE-Net: A Lightweight Attention-Based Residual Network for Covid-19 and Pneumonia Prediction Using Chest X-Ray
The COVID-19 has taken a lot of lives across the world and has left some with lifetime sufferings from some unbearable side-effects. It is climacteric to filter out COVID-19 and Viral Pneumonia by virtue of their homogeneous symptoms. Our motto is to relegate the chest X-Ray (CXR) of a patient into Normal (uninfected), Covid (SARS-CoV-2 infected) and Virus (viral pneumonia infected). We propose Lw-ResSASE-Net, a Light Weighted Residual Depthwise Separable Convolutional Neural Network integrated with our novel SASE (Spatial Attention-Squeeze and Excite) attention module for COVID-19 and Pneumonia prediction using X-Ray images of human chest. The Lw-ResSASE-Net comprises 7M parameters and offers approximately 98% state-of-art accuracy, precession, f1 score, and recall proving the superiority of our model over other related works. Besides, our proposed model was trained for 40 epochs and acquired its state-of-art accuracy in the 12th epoch only, without having any pretrained bases. Moreover, we have proposed SASE, a novel attention mechanism, working as the foundation of our proposed model.
